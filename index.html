I'm a software engineer with 7+ years of experience building software and turning ideas into products.

My career goal is to use technology to make people's lives better, solve problems I genuinely care about, and build cool things with fascinating people.

For the past four years, I've worked at adorsys, where I combine software engineering, architecture, and technical leadership. I lead an international team of 14 engineers, take projects from idea to production, and work with enterprise customers on digital identity, authentication, and the European Digital Identity Wallet ecosystem. I also contribute to technical consulting, business development, and industry events.

Outside of work, I love bringing my own ideas to life. I've launched SciCrumb, an iOS app that makes scientific research accessible, ColorMatchAI, a personal color analysis tool, and AgentsRumble.

In 2026, I also hosted a hands-on workshop at KeyConf26 in Prague on issuing verifiable credentials with Keycloak.
